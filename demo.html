What programs does TU Wien offer for bachelor students?
TU Wien offers a wide range of bachelor programs across engineering and technology. Key areas include:

• Computer Science (Informatik)
• Electrical Engineering
• Mechanical Engineering
• Civil Engineering
• Architecture
• Data Science & Statistics
• Business Informatics

Each program is research-oriented, taught in German (some in English), and typically lasts 6 semesters. For the full list, see the official study pages.
Source: tuwien.at — Studienangebot
When are the Welcome Days in 2026?
The Welcome Days at TU Wien for new students take place at the start of the winter semester 2026. Fresh students receive guidance on campus, university services, and how to get started with their studies.
